# Preserved source documents

These documents were moved from the docs bundle during the color-themes migration. Their bodies and attribution are retained, with relative links repaired for the archive. They are historical inputs, not normative specifications.

1. [Borealis](borealis.md): palette sources, role mappings, attribution, and hashes. Current requirements are in the color-themes main spec.
2. [Color themes](color-themes.md): original implementation design, illustrative types, and checklist. Superseded by the change design and current requirements.
3. [Rendering](rendering.md): upstream exploratory notes. Current viewport and collapse requirements are in toon-rendering and toon-navigation; abandoned proposals are not adopted.
4. [Search](search.md): upstream exploratory notes. Current search/navigation behavior is already specified in toon-navigation; suggestions about incremental search are not adopted.
5. [TOON review](toon-review.md): historical review of a removed codec patch. Current export boundaries remain in toon-display-extensions and the published codec guide.
